Course settings

Choose how students work through the course. The header and actions stay in place while this part scrolls.

Shuffle questions, show answers after the quiz and allow a second attempt are all set per course, and can be changed after you assign it.

Changes apply to students who have not started yet. Students already working keep the settings they began with.

Due dates are set per class when you assign the course.
